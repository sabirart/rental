Privacy & Google Data Use

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Rental Manager helps you manage rental properties, units, tenants, documents and payment records. Google sign-in is the only supported sign-in method.

Google account information

With your permission, Rental Manager receives your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile image (when provided) to authenticate you and display your owner profile. Your email is used as the verified account contact and is not a separate password-login credential.

Google Drive access and storage

Rental Manager requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. This allows the app to create and manage files it creates or that you explicitly open with the app; it is not blanket access to every file in your Drive. Rental Manager saves its live data in a folder named Rental Manager and recovery snapshots in Rental Manager Backups in your own Google Drive. The app reads and updates these files to provide the service and backup/restore features.

Data saved

Depending on how you use the app, saved data may include property and unit details, tenant contact details, lease-related notes, tenant documents you upload, payment records, settings, recycle-bin records and backup snapshots. Only add information and documents you are authorized to store.

How data is used

  • To authenticate your Google account and associate your records with that account.
  • To save, load, update, export and restore your rental-management records.
  • To maintain automatic live saving and daily recovery snapshots.
  • To troubleshoot reliability and security problems.

Sharing and sale

Rental Manager does not sell your Google user data or use it for advertising. Data is not shared with third parties except where needed to operate the service, comply with law, protect users, or when you choose to export or share a file yourself.

Security and retention

Google OAuth tokens are handled by the server and stored in HttpOnly cookies while the session is active; the app does not intentionally place access tokens in browser local storage. No internet service can promise absolute security. Your saved Drive files remain in your Google account until you delete them. Deleting app data in the product, if available, may not remove previously exported files or snapshots automatically.

Revoke access or delete data

You can revoke Rental Manager's access from your Google Account's third-party connections/security settings. You can also delete the Rental Manager and Rental Manager Backups folders from Google Drive. Before deleting, export a backup if you may need the records later.

Import and export

Exported backups may contain personal and financial information. Store them securely, and import only a backup you trust. When moving to another Google account, sign in to that account and use Settings → Account → Import Backup. Import replaces the current dataset after attempting to create a recovery snapshot, and imported records are saved to that account's own Drive.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the service operator using the contact details published with the deployed Rental Manager service.

This notice describes the intended product behavior. The service operator should review it for legal accuracy and publish accurate contact details before production use.
