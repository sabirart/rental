Privacy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Rental Manager helps you organize properties, tenants, and rent payments. We aim to keep your information under your control and explain the basics clearly.

Sign-in

You sign in with Google. The app uses basic account details such as your name, email address, and profile picture (if available) to identify your account and show your profile.

Your rental records

Information you enter—such as property details, tenant contact details, notes, documents, and payment records—is used to provide the features you choose to use. The app stores its working records and recovery backups in folders it creates in your own Google Drive. It requests access to files created by or opened with the app, not unrestricted access to every file in your Drive.

Your choices

You can export a copy of your records, remove app data using the available settings, or revoke the app's access in your Google Account security settings. Files you export or keep in Drive remain there until you delete them.

Sharing

Rental Manager does not sell your personal information or use it for advertising. Information is used to operate and protect the service, or shared when required by law or when you choose to share a file yourself.

Keeping your information safe

The app uses Google sign-in and server-side session handling to help protect access. No online service can guarantee perfect security, so only store information you are permitted to keep and protect any backup files you download.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the service operator through the contact details provided with the Rental Manager service.
